🗂️ Grouping Logic

Matching Rules

  • Zone-based matching, door-to-door delivery
  • Max 5 passengers per vehicle (sedan/SUV)
  • All drop points within ~3 km radius
  • Compatible arrival time windows

Fairness

  • Gender preference options available
  • Stable group recognition over time
  • Rotating drop order for fairness
💰 Pricing Model

Distance-Based Fares

  • Individual distance-based fares per rider
  • Swords (closest): €8
  • Malahide (mid): €10
  • Balbriggan (farthest): €14

Revenue

  • Driver earns combined total (~€35-40)
  • Weekly/monthly subscription passes
  • No split payments between riders

Assembly Point

The morning pickup
starts at a café.

Why a Restaurant?

Morning pickups use a nearby café as the assembly point — not a bus stop or street corner. Riders get shelter, seating, and a coffee while waiting. The venue gets guaranteed daily foot traffic. Safety improves, especially for women commuters at early hours.
